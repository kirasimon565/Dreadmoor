@diary ep01 1 ECHO
content:
I shouldn’t have gone there. Not that night. Not with them.

At first, it felt like nothing. Just noise, music, people pretending everything was normal. But something was off. I could feel it before anything actually happened.

Amelia kept insisting it was just a joke. A scare. Something to make the night more exciting. No one questioned her. No one ever does.

But when I stepped outside, everything changed. The air felt heavier, like the place itself was watching. I tried to laugh it off, but my hands wouldn’t stop shaking.

I remember checking my phone. The time didn’t make sense. It was later than it should have been. Or maybe earlier. I don’t know anymore.

I told myself I’d go back inside. Pretend everything was fine. But something in me said not to.

If anyone finds this… something happened in that factory. And they’re lying about it.
